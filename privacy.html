Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

lilC (“we”, “the app”) is a local C programming environment for iPhone, with a matching copy in the web browser. You write C in an editor and run it with PicoC, a small interpreter. This page is the public copy of the policy also summarized in Settings.

1. Data we collect

lilC does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or advertising identifiers. We do not use analytics, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs. These pages do not set cookies or load third-party scripts.

2. Data stored on your device

On iPhone, source files you create are stored in the app’s Documents folder. Theme, editor, and agent preferences are stored in UserDefaults. Agent conversation history and EDSGER teaching chats are stored on the iPhone. In the browser copy, files and theme stay in this browser (IndexedDB / localStorage). We do not receive those files or conversations.

3. How we use data

Local files are used to let you edit, run, share, and ask the on-device agent to work on programs. EDSGER chat history is used to continue your local learning conversations. Preferences are used to control the app’s appearance and agent mode.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent personal data. lilC runs C on-device with PicoC and runs its optional AI agent and EDSGER tutor on-device with a bundled model. Neither sends your code, questions, or conversations to a cloud AI.

5. Tracking

lilC does not track you across apps or websites. We do not use the App Tracking Transparency prompt because we do not track.

6. Children

lilC is a general-audience coding tool. It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your choices

You can delete an individual file from Home → Delete and erase local files from Settings. You can turn off Agent Mode in Settings. You can delete EDSGER conversations from its chat history. Uninstalling lilC removes its local files, agent history, and EDSGER chats from this device.

8. Security

Programs run in a constrained interpreter. system() is disabled. Do not paste secrets you would not keep on your phone.

9. Purchases

C lessons stay free. The iPhone app may offer a one-time (non-subscription) unlock for an offline Linux study course. Apple processes that purchase. This version of lilC does not sell a subscription.

10. Contact

The project is at github.com/garrettmichae1/lilc.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and the in-app copy.
